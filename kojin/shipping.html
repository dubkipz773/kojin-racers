Thank you for shopping with Kojin Racers. This policy explains how we make and ship your order and what to expect after you buy.

Order processing

Our tees are printed on demand, which means each one is made after you order it. Production typically takes several business days before your tee ships (longer during holidays and busy periods). We do not offer next-day or next-business-day shipping.

Garage gear (car accessories) is fulfilled by a separate supplier and may ship separately from tees, in its own package with its own tracking:

  • Items shipped from a US warehouse typically arrive in about a week.
  • Items shipped from overseas typically arrive in 1–2 weeks.

The expected timing for each accessory is listed on its product page. All timeframes are estimates, not guarantees, and can be affected by carrier delays, weather, customs, or other events outside our control.

Shipping methods and costs

Available shipping methods, costs, and estimated delivery times are calculated and shown at checkout based on your destination and the items in your cart. If your order includes items from more than one fulfillment partner, they may arrive on different days.

Shipping destinations

We ship within the United States. Tees can also be shipped to many other countries; the destinations and rates available for your order are shown at checkout. Garage gear currently ships to US addresses only.

Some accessories, especially battery-powered items such as jump starters, may have destination or shipping-method restrictions (for example, some Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, US territory, PO Box, or military addresses). We do not have a watt-hour rating or lithium shipping certification on file for the Jump Starter + Tire Inflator. Any such limits are noted on the product page or confirmed at checkout. If we can't ship an item to your address, we'll contact you and refund that item.

International orders

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, fees, and customs delays. Any charges collected at checkout are shown before you complete your purchase; any others are set by your country and are the recipient's responsibility.

Order tracking

When each package ships, we'll email you a confirmation with tracking information where the shipping method supports it. Tracking may take a day or two to update.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to correct it; because tees go into production quickly, we may not be able to change an order once production has started.

Lost, damaged, or delayed packages

If your order is lost, arrives damaged, or is significantly delayed, contact us and we'll help resolve it with the carrier or our fulfillment partner.
