Returns

You may request a return within 30 days after your item is delivered. Items must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition and packaging. We may reduce a refund if an item comes back used, damaged, or missing parts.

Printed-to-order tees

Our tees are printed on demand just for your order, so returns of tees for reasons like a change of mind or a size swap may be limited. Please check the size guide before ordering. If your tee has a print defect, is damaged, or isn't what you ordered, it's always covered (see below).

Return shipping

For approved returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective or damaged, or we made an error (such as a wrong item or a print defect). In those cases we'll cover the cost of fixing it.

How to start a return

Contact us before sending anything back. If your return is approved, we'll send instructions on how and where to send your package. Items sent back without an approved return request may not be accepted.

Cancellations

You can request to cancel or remove items from your order within 15 minutes after placing it by contacting us. After that, your order may already be in production or with our fulfillment partner, and we may not be able to cancel it, but contact us and we'll do what we can.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

Please inspect your order when it arrives and contact us within 30 days of delivery if an item is defective, damaged, or not what you ordered. Include your order number and a photo of the problem. We'll offer a replacement or a refund at no cost to you.

Garage gear

Accessories must be returned unused, with all parts, manuals, and original packaging. Battery-powered items may have extra shipping restrictions for returns; contact us first and we'll explain how to send them back.

Exchanges

Contact us to ask about exchange options before returning an item.

Refunds

We'll notify you after we receive and inspect your return and let you know whether the refund was approved. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank or payment provider may need additional time to post the refund. If you don't see it within a reasonable time after approval, contact us.

This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws, including rights that may apply if goods are defective, not as described, not delivered within the required or agreed timeframe, or otherwise do not comply with applicable law.
