Urgency & extraction rules

CRITICAL — SAFETY (rare)

  • Grinding brakes and soft/spongy pedal
  • Can’t stop / won’t stop / no brakes
  • Pulling hard while braking
  • Brake fluid leak
  • Explicit don’t-drive / tow / unsafe to drive

HIGH / HIGH — INSPECT

  • Separated exhaust / dragging pipe / cabin fumes
  • Serious drivability (intermittent no-go with CEL)
  • Brake pad/rotor jobs or squeaks → inspect note — not CRITICAL tow language

NORMAL

  • Alignment, oil change, how-to questions, rebuild quotes, general noise

Draft rules

  • Never say “column B/C/D”
  • Never claim a message was sent
  • Always end: “This is a draft for the shop to review; it has not been sent.”
